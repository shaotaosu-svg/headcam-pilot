技术支持

头控云台让你用 AirPods 的头部动作控制 Insta360 Luna Ultra 的云台：你转头，镜头就跟着转。

需要什么

  • iPhone，iOS 17 或更新版本
  • 支持空间音频头部追踪的 AirPods（AirPods 3/4、AirPods Pro、AirPods Max）
  • Insta360 Luna Ultra

快速开始

  1. 打开相机并放在 iPhone 旁边，打开 App，按提示允许蓝牙、本地网络和运动权限。
  2. 在首页点“连接”，第一次需要在相机上确认配对。
  3. 戴上 AirPods，点“开始头追”，看向相机保持不动完成对准，然后转头即可。
  4. 需要停止时，点“跟随中”按钮并选择“停止头追”，云台会停住。

锁屏跟随与旁白录音

开启“锁屏跟随”后，头追运行期间 App 会录制你的语音旁白，锁屏后也会继续跟随。录音在 App 的“旁白录音”列表里可以播放、分享或删除，7 天后自动删除。不开启时，头追只在屏幕亮着时工作。

安全提示

头部追踪会让相机转动。请牢固安装相机，保持转动路径无遮挡；如果相机动作异常，请立即在 App 中停止头追或直接关闭相机。

常见问题

找不到相机？

确认相机已开机、电量充足，iPhone 的蓝牙已打开，并关闭其他正在连接这台相机的 App。

头追不动？

确认 AirPods 已连接到这部 iPhone 并戴好，型号支持头部追踪；在“设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 运动与健身”中允许本 App。

What you need

  • iPhone with iOS 17 or later
  • AirPods with spatial audio head tracking (AirPods 3/4, AirPods Pro, AirPods Max)
  • Insta360 Luna Ultra

Getting started

  1. Turn on the camera near your iPhone, open the app and allow Bluetooth, Local Network and Motion when asked.
  2. Tap Connect on the first page. The first time, approve the pairing on the camera.
  3. Put on your AirPods, tap Start head tracking, look at the camera and hold still to aim, then turn your head.
  4. To stop, tap the Following button and choose Stop head tracking; the gimbal stops.

Lock-screen follow and narration

With lock-screen follow on, the app records your voice narration while head tracking runs and keeps following after you lock the iPhone. You can play, share or delete recordings in the app's Narration list; they are deleted after 7 days. With it off, head tracking works while the screen is on.

Safety

Head tracking moves the camera. Mount the camera securely and keep its path clear. If the camera behaves unexpectedly, stop head tracking in the app or turn the camera off.
