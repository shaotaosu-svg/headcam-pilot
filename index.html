HeadCam Pilot · 头控云台
iPhone App

转头，镜头就跟着转

头控云台用 AirPods 的头部动作控制 Insta360 Luna Ultra 的云台。戴上 AirPods，看向相机完成对准，之后你看向哪里，镜头就转向哪里。开启锁屏跟随后，还能边录语音旁白边拍，锁屏也继续跟随。

Turn your head, the camera follows

HeadCam Pilot uses AirPods head motion to aim the gimbal of an Insta360 Luna Ultra. Put on your AirPods, look at the camera to aim, and the camera follows where you look. With lock-screen follow on, it records your voice narration and keeps following after you lock the iPhone.
