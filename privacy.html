隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 18 日

头控云台（HeadCam Pilot）通过蓝牙和相机自带的 Wi-Fi 与你的相机通信。App 没有账号，没有统计分析，也没有广告；开发者不收集任何个人数据，App 也不会把数据发送给开发者或第三方服务器。

头部追踪

AirPods 的头部动作只在你的 iPhone 上处理，用于控制云台转向，不会被保存，也不会发送到任何地方。

麦克风与旁白录音

只有在你开启“锁屏跟随”后，App 才会在头追运行期间用 iPhone 麦克风录制语音旁白，锁屏后也会继续跟随。录音只保存在你的 iPhone 上，显示在 App 的“旁白录音”列表和“文件 › 头控云台 › Narration”中，7 天后自动删除，你也可以随时删除。录音不会被上传。

定位

仅在你添加相机 Wi-Fi 时使用，让 iOS 识别当前连接的 Wi-Fi 名称。位置信息不会被保存或共享。

蓝牙与本地网络

用于发现、配对和控制相机，接收相机的实时画面；也可以在附近两部手机之间共享实时画面。这些通信只在本地进行，不经过云端服务。

相机与照片

相机权限只用于接收外接 USB 采集设备的视频。照片权限只用于把你选择的片段保存到“照片”。

保存在你 iPhone 上的内容

  • 已保存的相机名称和最近使用的 Wi-Fi 名称；相机 Wi-Fi 密码保存在这部 iPhone 的钥匙串中。
  • 偏好设置、你导入的 .cube 文件和缓存的相机素材。
  • 每台已保存相机的上次实时画面，忘记相机时一并删除。
  • 诊断记录：只有当你选择“导出诊断信息”并主动分享时，才会离开这部 iPhone。

第三方

Apple 可能依据其自身政策处理权限提示和系统诊断信息。通过 TestFlight 测试时，Apple 可能依据其条款向开发者提供崩溃报告和你主动提交的反馈。保存片段或使用分享面板由你主动发起，之后由 Apple 或你选择的 App 处理该文件。

儿童

本 App 不面向儿童，也不收集任何人的个人数据。

Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

HeadCam Pilot talks to your camera over Bluetooth and the camera's own Wi-Fi. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. The developer does not collect personal data, and the app sends no data to the developer or to any third-party server.

Head tracking

AirPods head motion is processed on your iPhone only, to aim the gimbal. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

Microphone and narration

Only when you turn on lock-screen follow, the app records your voice narration with the iPhone microphone while head tracking runs, and keeps following after you lock the iPhone. Recordings stay on your iPhone, in the app's Narration list and in Files › HeadCam Pilot › Narration. They are deleted automatically after 7 days, and you can delete them any time. They are never uploaded.

Location

Used only when you add a camera's Wi-Fi, so iOS can tell the app which Wi-Fi this iPhone is on. Your location is not stored or shared.

Bluetooth and local network

Used to find, pair and control your camera and to receive its live picture, and optionally to share that picture with a nearby phone. This traffic stays on your local link; there is no cloud service.

Camera and Photos

Camera access is used only to receive video from an external USB capture device. Photos access is used only to save clips you choose.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Saved camera names and the last Wi-Fi name; the camera Wi-Fi password is kept in this iPhone's Keychain.
  • Preferences, .cube files you import and cached camera media.
  • The last live picture of each saved camera, removed when you forget the camera.
  • Diagnostic logs, which leave the iPhone only if you choose Export diagnostics and share them yourself.

Third parties

Apple may process permission prompts and system diagnostics under its own policies. During TestFlight testing, Apple may provide the developer with crash reports and feedback you choose to send, under Apple's terms. Saving a clip or using the share sheet is something you start; Apple or the app you pick then handles that file.

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no personal data from anyone.
